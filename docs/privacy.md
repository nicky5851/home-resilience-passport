## What we collect

When you create an account and add a home, we collect:

- Your email address and password.
- The address and postcode of each home you add.
- Your household answers: who holds the home (owner, landlord or renter), income band, budget,
  what matters most to you, and — only if you choose to tell us — whether anyone in your home is
  vulnerable (over 75, disabled, has a long-term illness, or has young children).
- Any certificates you upload to record completed work.
- Standard web server logs (never your password, cookies, tokens or full request bodies).

## Why we collect it

- To show you your home's climate risks, Resilience Score, and a costed improvement plan.
- To match you against funding, grants and discounts you may be eligible for.
- To keep a history of your score over time and the work you have recorded.

## What we never do

- We never send your email, address, postcode, house number, income band, budget, tenure or
  vulnerability answer to the AI model that writes your plan's explanations. It only ever sees
  property facts, hazard levels, score components and the measures themselves.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or scripts. The only cookie we set is
  the one that keeps you signed in, which is strictly necessary for the service to work.
- We never make your household answers public. A shared passport link shows only your address,
  score, risks, plan and whether you have completed work — never your household answers, your
  email or your certificate files.

## Your choices

- **Download your data:** from your Account page, at any time.
- **Delete your account:** from your Account page, at any time. This deletes your homes too.
- **Stop sharing a passport:** the public link stops working immediately.
- The vulnerability answer is entirely optional, defaults to "prefer not to say", and you can
  change it back at any time.

## Who to contact

If you have questions about this notice, contact the team running this project.
